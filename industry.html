Generative & multimodal AI

iSpot SAGE AI

SAGE AI is iSpot’s enterprise platform for multimodal creative analysis and advertising intelligence.

Agentic analysis

I architected and built core SAGE workflows that turn a natural-language question into an executable analysis: planning the steps, generating code, querying data, and returning results. I took this work from an end-to-end research prototype into production, including stress testing and model cost optimization.

Hierarchical theme discovery

I designed the hierarchical theme taxonomy and production pipelines that organize consumer feedback across ads, brands, categories, and industries. The system combines language models and clustering, with a taxonomy viewer for exploring themes and comparing creative work.

Creative effectiveness

Ace Metrix | an iSpot Company

Ace Metrix is iSpot’s creative assessment product, combining consumer research and creative benchmarking to evaluate video advertising.

Modeling emotional response

I trained a transformer model to classify emotional responses expressed in viewers’ written feedback on advertising, turning free-form language into structured measures for creative assessment.

I also developed census-derived survey-weighting methods and analyzed their effects on creative scores, bringing the revised methodology into production.

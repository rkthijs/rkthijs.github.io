Academic Background

Research

My mathematical research spans dynamical systems, ergodic theory, probability, and hyperbolic geometry, with both theoretical and computational work.

My earlier work in Boston University’s Computational Neuroscience Lab focused on neural network modeling and adaptive resonance theory, contributing to publications on memristive hardware and brain-computer interfaces.

See my publications in mathematics and computational neuroscience.

  • Packing Problems — Structure and visualization of horoball packings in hyperbolic space.
  • Complex Dynamics — Julia sets and the fractal geometry of iterated rational maps.

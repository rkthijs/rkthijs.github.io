Agentic & multimodal AI

iSpot SAGE AI

Principal Research Data Scientist · iSpot

I architected core agentic workflows for SAGE AI, iSpot’s platform for multimodal advertising analysis: translating a question into a plan, generating code, executing it against data, and returning results.

The NLP pipelines I designed supply SAGE with structured inputs: hierarchical themes extracted from consumer feedback and organized across ads, brands, categories, and industries.
